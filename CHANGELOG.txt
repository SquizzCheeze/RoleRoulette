RoleRoulette - Changelog
========================


V1.0 (2026-10-18)
-----

First release.

- Randomly picks who tanks, who heals and who DPSes, and a spec for each
  person from the ones they're willing to play (alts included).
- Works for one to five people: five get 1 tank / 1 healer / 3 DPS, fewer get
  a slice of that, for friends picking specs before queueing.
- Roster with Import Party, a spec picker per person, Re-roll, and Announce to
  party chat.
- Rotate roles: makes it much less likely anyone gets the same role as last
  game.
- If no group can be made, it says why ("Nobody has a healer spec in their
  pool").
- Same window and theme as SquizzFrames and Squizzumables, and part of the
  combined /squizz window when either is installed.
- /rr or /roleroulette.
